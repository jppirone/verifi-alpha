(a) Candidate data model
The base tables candidates, verification_items, email_verifications and verification_item_timeline are NOT created in any migration. They were made by hand in the dashboard (20260903000000_resume_pipeline.sql:1-7, 108-114). Their columns are known only from later ALTERs and code.
- Contact: printed values live per document on resume_documents (printed_header, candidate_location, candidate_phone, candidate_email; migrations 20260912030000, 20260912010000, 20260923040000). Account values live on candidates (email, phone, first_name, last_name, phone_verified_at, verified_phone_number, kyc_verified_at, personal_location, header_display_mode). Contact has no status column. "Verified" is implied by a timestamp being non null. The screen tags printed contact "Candidate-stated" (candidate.html:4191, 4246).
- Work: work_history_items (company, title, dates with precision year/month/day/present, location, heading, job_responsibilities, status, candidate_edited_fields, flag and staff block columns).
- Education: education_items, same pattern.
- Certifications and licenses: certification_items (name, issuing_body, dates, license_number, status, source_match, issuer_category, heading); license_items is 1:1 with a certification (20260918020000, 20260918030000:23-24) and carries state, outcome, reason, detail, attempts, recheck and report columns.
- Skills: skill_items (skill_text, position, heading). No status column by design (20260906020000:15-16).
- Summary, needs review, other sections: candidate_freeform_sections (section_type summary, hobbies_other, needs_review, additional_info, skills_secondary; heading; content). Summary variants: candidate_summary_versions (20260908030000).
- Customization: candidate_customization and candidate_item_overrides (20260921090000). They never store a verification status.
- Overlap: work_overlap_holds (action pending/explained/ignored, explanation, source, resolved_by, resolution_note) and work_overlap_pairs (overlap_days; threshold more than 30 days, confirm-resume-data/index.ts:131), migration 20260927060000.
- Where validation status is stored:
  1) work_history_items.status, education_items.status, certification_items.status: text, default "Not Submitted for Verification". The intended words are in a comment only (20260903000000:95-97). No check constraint. Nothing writes to it after insert, and no screen or employer path reads it.
  2) verification_items.status is the real status. Text with no check constraint (20260906030000:140-143). Linked by source_item_id. Values in use: New, In Progress, Awaiting Response, Needs Reconciliation, Confirmed, Discrepancy, Verification Not Possible, Unable to Verify, Resolved. Date: status_changed_at (trigger). Reason: free text in internal_note, automated_check, timeline note, and found_value for a Discrepancy. There is no structured reason column. Set by confirm-resume-data on insert, by staff through update-verification-item (STATUS_VALUES :41), and for licenses by verify-license and apply_license_downgrade.
  3) license_items.verification_outcome (verified, ambiguous, unsupported_jurisdiction, not_found, error, incomplete) plus verification_reason and verified_at. This is the only structured reason code today.
